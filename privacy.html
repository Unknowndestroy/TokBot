Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This policy covers data collected by TokBot while it operates in a Discord server that has enabled its protection features. It does not cover Discord itself, which has its own privacy policy. For a shorter, plain-language summary, see the Transparency page. Using TokBot also means accepting our Terms of Service.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to members of Discord servers where TokBot is active, and to anyone who interacts with TokBot directly, for example by opening a support ticket or submitting a ban appeal. Server owners choose whether to enable TokBot's protection features and what punishments to apply; TokBot only stores data related to security enforcement, not general server activity.

2. Data we collect

TokBot only stores data connected to a security detection, ban, appeal, or support ticket. Users who have never triggered a flag do not have a record in our permanent ban databases.

  • Discord user ID and username, to identify and maintain blacklist entries.
  • The server ID where a detection occurred, so staff can investigate raids or repeated incidents.
  • The message ID (and, in some flagged cases, the message content) that triggered a detection, such as a scam link.
  • Perceptual hashes of images flagged as scams. We store the mathematical hash used for matching, not the original image file.
  • For accounts flagged as "Hacked," a temporary backup of the member's roles, so permissions can be restored once the account is secured and an appeal is granted.
  • Notes staff add to a user's case during moderation, tickets, or appeals.
  • Ticket and appeal conversation transcripts, archived for quality assurance.
  • A unique Ban ID linking a case to its appeal ticket.
  • If a previously banned user is flagged again, the earlier case is kept in that user's history rather than deleted.
  • Aggregate detection counts (e.g. how many scams were blocked on a given day), which are not tied to any individual user.

3. How we use this data

  • To detect and act on known scammers and malicious bot accounts across every server TokBot protects.
  • To let a flagged user appeal a ban or hacked-account flag and have staff review the specific case.
  • To help server staff investigate raids, repeat offenders, and specific scam campaigns.
  • To monitor overall detection performance using aggregate, non-identifying statistics.

4. Data sharing

Blacklist and detection data is shared across all servers that have TokBot's protection enabled, so a user flagged in one server is automatically recognized in others. We do not sell data, and we do not share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Our database is private and accessible only to authorized bot developers and high-level administrators.

5. Data retention

Data tied to a ban, appeal, or ticket is retained as part of that user's case history so repeat incidents can be tracked accurately. If you believe your data should be reviewed or removed, contact us using the details below.

6. Your options

  • You can appeal a ban or hacked-account flag directly through TokBot's ticket system.
  • You can request a copy of, or the deletion of, data tied to your case by contacting us through our support server.
  • If your appeal is granted, associated punishment data tied to the resolved case is updated to reflect that outcome.

7. Children's privacy

TokBot operates within Discord and follows Discord's own age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from users below Discord's minimum age requirement outside of what Discord itself already collects.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TokBot's features change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of TokBot after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your data, can be sent through our support server.
